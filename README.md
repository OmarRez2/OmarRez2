## Flagship: Meridian

<a href="https://omarrez2.github.io/meridian-intelligence/">
<picture>
  <source media="(prefers-color-scheme: light)" srcset="assets/meridian/meridian-light.svg" />
  <img src="assets/meridian/meridian-dark.svg" width="100%" alt="Meridian, WWI Supply Chain Intelligence: a bilingual analytics experience with an Analytics Lab and a Decision Simulator, built on 41 months of reconciled records." />
</picture>
</a>

**The business problem.** A wholesaler's sales, purchasing and inventory records didn't agree. Meridian reconciles 41 months of Wide World Importers data, with 663 customers and 227 stock items, into one model. Its totals match the team's SQL benchmarks exactly. It then lets a manager explore the data:

- **Analytics Lab.** Every statement is labelled *finding*, *calculation*, *hypothesis* or *recommendation*, and recomputed from the data at render time.
- **Decision Simulator.** Price, volume, supplier-shock and stock-cover scenarios. Each one shows a profit bridge whose steps add up exactly, a break-even point, sensitivity, and an A/B comparison. Results are presented as scenario arithmetic, not forecasts.
- **Built for real use.** English and Arabic (RTL), light and dark themes, keyboard support, zero axe-core violations, 81 automated data tests, and performance budgets.
